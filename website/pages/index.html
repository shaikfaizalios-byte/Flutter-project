Everything you need between “I have the papers” and exam day

Built around real previous papers, with every uncertain result flagged for you to check, never quietly “fixed”.

Import your papers

PDFs with a text layer or scanned pages (English and Hindi OCR). Questions, options, answer keys and passages are found automatically.

Diagrams kept as printed

Venn diagrams, figure series, mirror images, charts and formulas are cut out of the paper and shown exactly as printed, in review and in practice.

You stay in control

Unclear text, missing answers or lost figures are marked for review with the reason. Topic suggestions show why they were made. Nothing counts until you confirm it.

Repeated models

See how many of your papers each topic and question type appeared in, year by year, and practise exactly that type with one tap.

Repeat Radar opt-in

Counts pooled across learners who choose to share, published only when at least two learners reviewed the same exam shift and agree. No question text is ever shared.

Practice and mock tests

Untimed practice by subject, topic or question type with explanations. Timed mocks use only officially verified exam patterns, never guessed ones.

Revision that sticks

Flashcards on a spaced schedule, a daily plan that explains each item, a reminder at your time, and progress based on your real answers.

AI, clearly labelled optional

Draft explanations, similar practice questions or a topic list for a new exam. AI content is always labelled and never presented as an official question.

Questions

Does PrepLoop come with previous papers?

No. You import the papers you have (for example from official sources). PrepLoop organises them; it doesn't distribute papers.

Which exams are supported?

Any multiple-choice exam. Several exams are in the list, and you can create your own with its subjects and topics. Questions with numeric or written answers aren't supported yet.

Are the “repeated” counts predictions?

No. They describe the papers imported by you (or, for the Radar, by learners who share their counts). They are not a forecast of what an exam will contain.

Is AI required?

No. Everything works without AI. Where AI is used it is optional, clearly labelled and reviewed by you before it counts.

Does it work offline?

Notes, flashcards and revision work offline in the apps, and practice answers are saved and sent later. Importing papers and starting sessions need a connection.

Is PrepLoop affiliated with SSC, IBPS, RRB, UPSC or NTA?

No. PrepLoop is an independent study tool.
